# ArcSprout UI Thesis v2

## Direction

ArcSprout should feel like a clean, professional membership system for the Arc economy. The visual language should align with Arc's official positioning as an economic OS: stablecoin-native, deterministic, transparent, and built for real participation.

## Brand Reading

- Arc is not a neon crypto brand.
- Arc is an infrastructure brand.
- The community layer should feel like Arc House: visible progress, contribution recognition, and clear paths for deeper involvement.
- ArcSprout should borrow the language of community programs, tiers, events, and recognition rather than mascot-driven fantasy.

## Design Principles

1. Use deep navy, slate, silver-white, and Arc blue as the core palette.
2. Keep green only for success, progress, and active status.
3. Use large typography, but keep the composition quiet and well spaced.
4. Prefer editorial layout over decorative visuals.
5. Use data, state, and progression as the main visual content.
6. Keep all sections modular so new utility can be added without redesigning the page.

## Product Structure

### Home

- Hero: one clear statement about ArcSprout as Arc membership / passport infrastructure.
- Proof block: network, fee model, community, status.
- Arc House block: transparent progression and contribution recognition.
- Architects block: tiered recognition and benefits.
- Agentic Economy block: future AI-native utility and onchain services.
- Future module slots: reserved areas for new products and campaigns.

### Enter

- Hero: wallet check and score gate.
- Status panel: score, network, eligibility, last transaction.
- State machine: visitor, ready, wrong network, evaluating, eligible, minting, success, not eligible, error.
- Score breakdown: signal categories and live metrics.
- Post-mint state: what the user gets next.

## What Not To Do

- Do not use mascot-heavy visuals.
- Do not use cyber-neon gradients as the primary identity.
- Do not overdecorate with glow, chrome, or gamer-style effects.
- Do not make the page feel like a dashboard mockup.
- Do not hide important status behind vague copy.

## Success Criteria

- The interface looks calm, premium, and credible.
- The Arc logo shape and palette feel native to the page.
- The community story is obvious within the first few seconds.
- The UI can absorb future utilities without structural rewrites.
- Mint flow states are clear and never ambiguous.
